This policy covers Offerloop at {{site}} ("Offerloop", "we"). Offerloop is in early access and run by a small team; questions go to {{contact}}.

## What we collect

**Your account.** Your email address, and a password (stored only as a secure hash by our sign-in provider, Supabase) or your Google sign-in.

**What you put in.** Your career profile (role, skills, cities, experience, pay expectations, deal-breakers), your resume and the versions you tailor, your workflows and their results, your Tracker, your DSA progress, your Learn list and study notes, and your Signal alert settings.

**What your tools report.** If you use the Hirist agent: which approved jobs it applied to and the outcome. If you connect Gmail: the sender and subject of job-related emails (never the email body), to update your Tracker.

**Basic technical data.** Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (IP address, time, page requested) for security and debugging.

## What we don't collect

- **Your AI keys** (Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq) stay in your browser and are sent only with each AI request; we never store them.
- **Your Hirist password or session** never leave your computer.
- **We don't sell your data**, show ads, or use your data to train AI models.

## Why we use it

Only to provide Offerloop: find and score jobs for you, run your workflows, apply where you approved, keep your Tracker and resume, and send the alerts you asked for. Pay figures from public job postings are pooled anonymously to estimate market pay; nothing from your profile is shared with other users.

## Who processes it

- **Supabase** — sign-in and the database where your data is stored.
- **Render** — hosts the website and server.
- **Google** — only if you sign in with Google or connect Gmail.
- **Your AI provider** — receives the resume or job text you send when you use Resume Studio or Learn, under that provider's terms.
- **CallMeBot** — only if you turn on Signal alerts; it relays the message text (job titles and links).
- **Hirist** and company career sites — we read their public job listings; the Hirist agent on your PC applies there in your name when you approve.

## Google user data

If you connect Gmail, Offerloop requests read-only access (`gmail.readonly`) and uses it only to find job-related emails and update your Tracker. The access token is kept in an encrypted cookie in your browser. Offerloop's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can disconnect at any time on the Tracker or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

## How long we keep it

As long as your account exists. Workflow history and the Hirist queue are trimmed automatically. When you delete your account, we delete everything stored for it.

## Your choices

You can see and change your data in the app at any time, turn off Gmail, Signal alerts and the Hirist agent, and ask {{contact}} to export or delete your account and data.

## Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS), sign-in is handled by Supabase, secrets you give us (like your CallMeBot key) are encrypted before they're stored, and each person's data is kept separate. No system is perfectly secure; tell {{contact}} if you find a problem.

## Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll say so in the app.
