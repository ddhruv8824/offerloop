The **Jobs for you** feed on your Dashboard searches live openings and ranks them against your profile. Nothing here is sample data: every job links to the real posting.

## Where jobs come from

- **Hirist** — searched by your target role and cities.
- **30+ company career pages** — read through their public job feeds (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters), filtered to roles in India or remote roles open to India.

The same role on Hirist and on the company's own page is shown once, with *also on Hirist*. Open **Sources** under the search summary to see how many roles each source returned and which (if any) didn't answer.

## How a job is scored

Every job gets a score out of 100 and a verdict:

| Verdict | Score |
| --- | --- |
| Strong fit | 80+ |
| Good fit | 65–79 |
| Worth a try | 50–64 |
| Long shot | under 50 |

The score combines four areas. Click **Why 72%?** on a job to see each one, with the reasons for and against:

| Area | Weight | What it checks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Skills | 45 | How many of the skills the job asks for are on your profile. Mandatory skills count double. |
| Role | 25 | Whether it's the kind of role you want (e.g. a *Frontend* role for a *Frontend Developer*). |
| Experience | 15 | The years the posting asks for against yours. Being 2+ years short caps the score. |
| Location | 15 | One of your cities, or remote and open to India. |

**Pay** is shown but doesn't change the score — most postings don't state it. Instead, a job that states pay below your minimum is hidden (see below).

### Role first: when new tools shouldn't count against you

Tech moves fast; a job may ask for a framework you haven't used yet but could learn in a week. In an [automation's](/docs/automations) **Find new jobs** step, set **New tools in the JD** to **Don't count — add to Learn**. Then:

- the score leans on the role, experience and location (skills 15, role 45, experience 20, location 20);
- skills you have still lift the score; ones you don't can't drag it down;
- the tools you're missing go on your [Learn](/docs/learn) list when you approve the job.

## Deal-breakers and minimum pay

Deal-breakers from your profile **hide** jobs instead of just warning you:

- **Known ones** — night or rotational shifts, service bonds, relocation, 6-day weeks, services companies — are spotted in the job description.
- **Remote only** hides jobs that aren't remote.
- **Your own** (e.g. *No crypto*) hide a job when its title or company names it; if only the description mentions it, you get a warning instead.
- **Minimum pay** hides jobs whose posting states a top salary below your minimum.

Nothing disappears silently: open **N jobs hidden by your deal-breakers** below the feed to see them and why.

## Check a job from anywhere

Found a job somewhere else? Paste its link into **Check this job** at the top of the feed to score it against your profile the same way.

## Filters

Filter the feed by text (title, company, city or skill), minimum match, source, remote, or hide jobs already on your Tracker. **Track** adds a job to your [Tracker](/docs/tracker); **Tailor** opens [Resume Studio](/docs/resume-studio).
