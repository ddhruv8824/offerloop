The **Tracker** is one board for every application: *Saved → Applied → In review → Assessment → Interviewing → Offer*, plus *Rejected*.

## How cards get there

- **Track** on any job in the [Job Finder](/docs/jobs);
- **Add to my tracker** in an [automation](/docs/automations) — approved jobs appear the next time you open Offerloop;
- the [Hirist agent](/docs/hirist-agent) — applied jobs move to *Applied*; jobs that need you get the next step *Finish applying on Hirist*;
- **Add job** on the Tracker, for anything else.

Open a card for its timeline (what happened, when, and from where), notes, the next step, and the original email if it came from Gmail. Change its status from the card.

Your Tracker is saved to your account and follows you to any device.

## Connect Gmail

Connect Gmail (read-only) and Offerloop looks at the sender and subject of your job-related emails — never the email body — to spot application confirmations, assessment links, interview invites, offers and rejections, and updates your cards:

- **Clear cases** (e.g. an interview invite from a company you applied to) move the card for you.
- **Unclear ones** show up as suggestions to accept or dismiss.
- Newsletters, job alerts and digests are ignored.

Every update links to the email (**Open in Gmail**). Offerloop only reads; it never sends, deletes or changes anything in your mailbox. Click **Disconnect** any time.

> **Early access:** Google limits apps that read Gmail to 100 approved testers until they pass Google's security review. If **Connect Gmail** says access is blocked, ask the person who invited you to add your Google account as a tester. Everything else on the Tracker works without Gmail.
