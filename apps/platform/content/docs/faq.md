## Is Offerloop free?

Yes, during early access. You bring your own AI key for Resume Studio and Learn (Gemini and Groq have free tiers).

## Does it apply to jobs without asking me?

No. Every workflow stops at **Wait for my approval**, and the Hirist agent only applies to jobs you approved.

## Can it apply on LinkedIn, Naukri or company websites?

Not yet: the agent applies on **Hirist** (one-click applications). Jobs from company career pages are added to your Tracker to apply on the company's site, with your tailored resume from [Resume Studio](/docs/resume-studio).

## Will my Hirist account get banned?

Automated applying may go against Hirist's terms. The agent stays slow (45–120 seconds between applications), capped (15 a day), stops at any CAPTCHA, and only applies to jobs you approved — which keeps the risk low, but it's your account and your decision.

## Do I need a Windows PC?

Only for the [Hirist agent](/docs/hirist-agent). Everything else works in any browser, on any device, including phones.

## Why didn't I get a Signal message?

Check the **Signal alerts** card on Automations: it shows how the last message went and why. See [Signal alerts](/docs/signal#when-a-message-doesn-t-arrive).

## Why is a job I expected missing?

It may be below your workflow's minimum match, hit one of your deal-breakers (see *N jobs hidden by your deal-breakers* under the feed), or already be applied to or queued. A scheduled run also only shows jobs that are new since the last run.

## Why does the site sometimes take a minute to load?

Early access runs on a small free server. After a deploy it can take about a minute to wake up.

## How do I invite a friend?

During early access, invites come from the admin. Ask the person who invited you.

## Something's broken / I have an idea

Tell the person who invited you — early access is exactly for this.
