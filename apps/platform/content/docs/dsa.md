**DSA Prep** gives you the coding questions most often asked by a company, or for a kind of role, in the order worth practicing them.

- **By company** — pick a company (the ones on your Tracker are listed first) to see its most-asked questions.
- **By domain** — must-know lists for your target role (e.g. frontend, backend, data).
- **My list** — questions you saved.

Filter by difficulty and topic, and hide what you've solved. Each question opens on LeetCode; when you come back, Offerloop asks whether you solved it and keeps your progress — saved to your account, on every device.

Automations with the **Pick DSA questions** step point you to the right lists for the companies in a run; the **Interview prep** template does it for one job you paste.
