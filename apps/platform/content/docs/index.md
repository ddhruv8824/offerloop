Offerloop is a workspace for switching jobs. It finds roles that fit you every morning, asks for your approval before it does anything, applies on Hirist from your own computer, and keeps your tracker, resume and interview prep in one place.

## The daily loop

Once it's set up, a normal day looks like this:

1. **09:00** — your workflow searches Hirist and 30+ company career pages and scores every job against your profile.
2. **09:01** — you get a Signal message with the best matches. Tap **Approve all**, or approve just the ones you like.
3. **Within 15 minutes** — the Hirist agent on your PC applies to the approved Hirist jobs, from your own logged-in browser.
4. **Later** — results land on your Tracker, Gmail replies move cards along, and new tools those jobs ask for go on your Learn list.

The only thing you do is approve.

## What Offerloop never does

- **It never applies without your approval.** Every application starts with your yes.
- **It never messages recruiters** or anyone else on your behalf.
- **It never sees your Hirist password.** The agent applies from a browser on your PC that you log in to yourself.
- **It never invents things on your resume.** Every AI edit is checked against your own career history, and you accept or reject each one.
