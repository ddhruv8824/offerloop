## 1. Create your account

Offerloop is invite-only during early access. You need an **invite code** (it looks like `OL-1A2B-3C4D`) from whoever invited you.

- **Email and password:** open **Sign up**, enter your email, a password (8+ characters) and your invite code.
- **Google:** click **Continue with Google**. After signing in, Offerloop asks for your invite code once.

You only enter the code once. After that, just log in.

## 2. Set up your profile

Right after signing up you land on the profile setup. It takes about two minutes:

1. **About you** — your name, and optionally your resume (PDF or Word). Offerloop reads your name, current role, years of experience and skills from it, so most of the next steps fill themselves in.
2. **Your next role** — the role you want (e.g. *AI Engineer*), the cities you'd work in (add *Remote* if you're open to it), your years of experience, your minimum pay in LPA, and optionally what you earn now (only used to suggest what to ask for).
3. **Your skills** — pick from suggestions for your role, type to search, or accept the ones found in your resume.
4. **Deal-breakers** — things you won't accept (night shifts, service bonds, relocation, 6-day weeks, services companies, remote only) or your own (e.g. *No crypto*). Jobs that hit one are hidden from your matches.

You can change all of this later under **Career Profile**.

> Your profile is saved to your account, so it follows you to any device you log in on.

## 3. See your matches

Open the **Dashboard**. The **Jobs for you** feed shows live roles ranked by how well they fit. Click **Why 72%?** on any job to see the breakdown. More in [Job Finder](/docs/jobs).

## 4. Set up the daily workflow

1. Go to **Automations** and pick the **Apply on Hirist** template.
2. Check the **Find new jobs** step: *From* is **Hirist only**, *Minimum match* around **60–70%**.
3. Keep the schedule at **every day at 09:00** (your time zone), and save.
4. Click **Run now** to try it. When it stops at **Wait for my approval**, tick the jobs you want and click **Approve**.

More in [Automations](/docs/automations).

## 5. Optional, but recommended

- **[Hirist agent](/docs/hirist-agent)** — so approved Hirist jobs are applied to automatically from your PC (Windows).
- **[Signal alerts](/docs/signal)** — so you can approve from your phone.
- **[Resume Studio](/docs/resume-studio)** — upload your resume once, then tailor it to any job in a minute.
- **[Gmail](/docs/tracker#connect-gmail)** — so replies to your applications update your Tracker.
