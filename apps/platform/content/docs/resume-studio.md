Resume Studio rewrites your resume for one job description at a time. The AI proposes small, specific edits; a second AI pass reviews them like a recruiter; Offerloop checks every edit against your real career history; and you accept or reject each one.

## Set up your AI key

Resume Studio uses an AI model you choose: **Google Gemini**, **OpenAI**, **Anthropic (Claude)** or **Groq**. Open **AI settings** in Resume Studio, pick a provider, paste your API key and click **Verify**.

- Your key stays **in your browser** (cleared when the tab closes, unless you tick *Remember on this device*). It's sent with each request and never stored on the server.
- Gemini and Groq have free tiers that are plenty for personal use.

The same settings are used for [Learn](/docs/learn) study notes.

## Your resume and master profile

Upload your resume (PDF or Word) once. Offerloop reads it into a **master profile**: every job, project, the tools you used in each, and notes you add. This is the source of truth — the AI may only use what's in it.

Add things the resume doesn't say (e.g. *"I also used Azure at Lyft"*) in the master profile or by telling the chat; they become usable in tailoring.

## Tailor to a job

Paste a job description and click **Tailor**. Offerloop:

1. **Analyzes the job** — must-have and nice-to-have skills and keywords, the way an applicant-tracking system (ATS) sees them.
2. **Drafts edits** — reworded bullets, a new summary, a reordered skills list.
3. **Reviews them like a recruiter** — a second AI pass keeps, improves or drops each edit, and may add up to 3 for must-haves your profile backs but the resume still hides. Its notes appear under *Recruiter review*.
4. **Checks honesty** — edits that use a number or skill your profile doesn't show are flagged on their card. Company names, titles, dates and education can't be changed at all.
5. **Scores it** — your ATS keyword match before and after.

Then review each edit card: **Accept**, **Reject**, or click it to see it on the resume. **Open questions** ask about skills the job wants that your profile doesn't show — answer *Yes, I have* (with a sentence on how) and re-tailor, or *No*.

Each job description gets its own **version**, saved to your account. Download any version as a PDF.

## From an automation

A workflow with **Tailor my resume** tailors this resume to every job you approve and saves each one as a version here, while the tab is closed. See [Tailored resumes](/docs/automations#tailored-resumes).

## Chat

Ask for changes in plain words: *"make the Stripe bullets punchier"*, *"what's missing for this job?"*, *"put Kafka first in skills"*. The same honesty rules apply.

## What it won't do

It won't add a skill, tool or number you don't have. If a job asks for something you haven't used, it asks you about it instead — and the tool goes on your [Learn](/docs/learn) list when you approve such a job in an automation.
